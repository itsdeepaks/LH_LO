# North star

Healthcare revenue-cycle teams can see whether claims were reimbursed as expected, understand why reimbursement differs, and know which claims deserve attention next.

## Primary audience

Independent and multi-location medical practices first; billing & RCM organizations and health systems second.

## Product framing

Liquidity Health is a claims intelligence platform. It connects claim, remittance, payment and contract evidence at the claim level to surface potential underpayments, denials, contract variance, payer/coding patterns and review priorities.

## Website conversion

A qualified healthcare operator asks Liquidity Health to analyze their claims or requests a product conversation.

## Do not position as

- a treasury platform
- a generic payments company
- a bank or lender
- a generic AI platform
- a denial-only product
